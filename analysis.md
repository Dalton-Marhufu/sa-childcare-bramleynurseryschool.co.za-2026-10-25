## Score: 3/10
- Visual design and hierarchy: 0
- Clarity of offer and services: 1
- CTA strength and conversion flow: 0
- Trust signals and credibility: 2
- Mobile usability and general polish: 0

## Top 3 Issues
- Dated, template-style layout with almost no visual hierarchy — no hero, weak headings, and content buried in dense paragraphs.
- No clear calls to action: enquiries are pushed into a generic contact form with no "Book a tour" or "Enquire" prompts visible while browsing.
- Programmes, fees, terms and enrolment are scattered across separate pages with 2018-era news items still on the homepage, making the offer feel stale and hard to understand.

## Top 3 Improvements
- A warm, photo-led hero built from the school's own gallery images with a clear headline and a prominent "Book a Tour" call to action.
- A dedicated Programmes section presenting the half-day and full-day options (with times) plus age-tailored groups, so parents instantly understand what's offered.
- Streamlined trust and contact: "Since 1952" credentials, enrichment activities, a gallery of real school life, and phone/email/address repeated at every scroll depth.
